Interactive 2

Wound-closure animation

Watch the fibroblast density n(r,t) advance across the wound bed. Switch between an untreated chronic wound and a treated one, and change how long the treatment lasts. A pulse that is too short lets the front advance and then recede.

Day 0.0: front at 1.97 mm

Static version: Figure 9. Every rate comes from the Tier-4 closure functions of the constants register (chronic rp = 0.50, d = 0.51 d−1; treated rp = 1.11, d = 0.45 d−1), and the drug effect relaxes with an 18 h time constant after the pulse ends. The animation stops when the front reaches the centre, because the model describes a chronic wound bed.
