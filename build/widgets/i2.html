Interactive 2

Wound-closure animation

Watch the fibroblast density n(r,t) advance across the wound bed. Apply a fresh patch on a schedule and watch each one top up the drug effect, compare a single long treatment pulse, or leave the wound untreated. Too little drug lets the front advance and then recede.

Day 0.0: front at 1.97 mm

Static versions: Figures 9 and 10. In Patches mode each patch is the 500 µm alginate patch of Interactive 1, which keeps tissue above target for about 7.2 h; the drug effect then fades with an 18 h time constant until the next patch (green saw-tooth on the timeline). The longest interval that still closes is about 21.8 h, so a daily change stalls just short. Every rate comes from the Tier-4 closure functions of the constants register (chronic rp = 0.50, d = 0.51 d−1; treated rp = 1.11, d = 0.45 d−1). The animation stops when the front reaches the centre, because the model describes a chronic wound bed.
